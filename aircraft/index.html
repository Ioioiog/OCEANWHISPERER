Robinson R66 NxG

A five-seat helicopter powered by a Rolls-Royce RR300 turbine. Four guests travel beside your captain, in a cabin built for comfort and calm.

01

Rolls-Royce RR300 turbine

The R66 is powered by the proven Rolls-Royce RR300 turbine, the heart of the industry’s best-selling light single-turbine helicopter.

02

Impact-resistant windshield

An impact-resistant windshield up front, standard on the R66 NxG, and the widest view of the coastline ahead.

03

Garmin glass cockpit

A full Garmin glass cockpit gives your captain clear, modern instrumentation for every phase of the flight.

04

Autopilot

An autopilot, standard on the R66 NxG, helps keep the flight smooth and steady while your captain guides the route.

05

Hand-stitched leather seats

Five seats in hand-stitched leather: your captain and four guests, never more.

06

Air-conditioned cabin

Air conditioning with an overhead vent for each seat, for cool, comfortable flights in the Caribbean heat.

Safety, in experienced hands.

Founded and flown by career aviation professionals, with decades of flight operations, command and safety leadership between them.

Our approach to safety
  1. 01

    Every route personally reviewed

    Timing, light and routing are planned in advance, flight by flight.

  2. 02

    Every flight personally briefed

    Your captain walks you through the route and the moments to watch for before the rotors turn.

  3. 03

    Built with safety in mind

    An impact-resistant windshield, a modern glass cockpit and an autopilot come standard on the R66 NxG.

  4. 04

    The captain has the final word

    Every flight is subject to weather and pilot-in-command authority. If conditions aren’t right, we wait.
