Pre-launch.

Ocean Whisperer is assembling the Inaugural List ahead of its first season. No reservations are being taken at this time, and no charges or commitments are collected through this site.

When reservations open, Inaugural List members will be notified personally, and full booking terms — covering scheduling, adjustments, weather considerations, and safety authority — will be provided at that time.

Our commitments, now and always.

Safety takes precedence over every other consideration. When Ocean Whisperer begins operating, the Pilot-in-Command will hold final authority over whether a flight operates, is delayed, or is rerouted. This authority is absolute and is exercised solely in the interest of guest and crew safety.

All flights will be conducted in accordance with applicable Curaçao Civil Aviation Authority regulations, with full commercial liability coverage appropriate to private helicopter operations.

Privacy.

Details shared through the Inaugural List form are held in confidence. We do not share, sell, or use them for purposes beyond personally responding to your interest in Ocean Whisperer.

Closing.

We appreciate the patience of Inaugural List guests as the aircraft is prepared, the crew trained, and the operation readied. Everything worth doing quietly is worth doing properly.
