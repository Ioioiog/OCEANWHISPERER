A turbine, made for the island.

We begin with the Robinson R66 NxG, a five-seat helicopter powered by a Rolls-Royce RR300 turbine. Four guests travel beside your captain in hand-stitched leather seats, with a Garmin glass cockpit, autopilot and an impact-resistant windshield up front.

As our missions grow, a larger six-to-seven seat aircraft will join the fleet.
